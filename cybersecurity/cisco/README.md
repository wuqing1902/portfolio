# Cisco Cybersecurity

## Overview

This repository documents my structured learning journey through **Cisco Cybersecurity**, with an emphasis on understanding cybersecurity principles, threat landscapes, data protection, organizational security operations, and professional cybersecurity practices.

The portfolio contains structured theory notes covering cybersecurity fundamentals, cyber threats and attack techniques, data and privacy protection, organizational security technologies, incident response, ethical considerations, and cybersecurity career development.

The primary objective of this repository is to develop a strong foundational understanding of cybersecurity and to connect theoretical security concepts with practical defensive strategies, risk management principles, and real-world security operations.

## Repository Navigation

### [Theory](/cybersecurity/cisco/theory.md)

The theory section contains structured notes summarizing the major cybersecurity concepts covered throughout the Cisco Cybersecurity learning program. The material progresses from foundational cybersecurity principles and the CIA triad to personal data protection, cyber threats, attackers, malware, social engineering, denial-of-service attacks, system vulnerabilities, and emerging threats. It then examines practical approaches to protecting data and privacy through network security, encryption, backups, authentication, multi-factor authentication, VPNs, and secure online practices. The later modules focus on organizational security, including firewalls, IDS/IPS, SIEM, DLP, network monitoring, penetration testing, risk management, and incident response, before concluding with cybersecurity law and ethics, professional responsibilities, industry certifications, and career development. Together, these topics provide a broad foundation in cybersecurity principles, defensive security practices, organizational security operations, and professional development.

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this learning program, I developed a stronger foundational understanding of cybersecurity across both technical and organizational perspectives.

Key outcomes include:

* Understanding cybersecurity as a risk management discipline rather than solely a technical function.
* Applying the CIA triad to evaluate confidentiality, integrity, and availability risks.
* Classifying common malware families and understanding their propagation and impact.
* Identifying common social engineering and network-based attack techniques.
* Understanding how software and hardware vulnerabilities can be exploited.
* Applying practical principles for protecting devices, networks, data, and online identities.
* Understanding the role of encryption, authentication, backups, and access control in data protection.
* Understanding how firewalls, IDS/IPS, SIEM, and DLP contribute to defense-in-depth architectures.
* Understanding the incident response lifecycle from detection through recovery.
* Recognizing the importance of continuous monitoring, testing, and risk management.
* Applying ethical and legal considerations when evaluating cybersecurity activities.
* Developing an initial cybersecurity career and certification roadmap.
